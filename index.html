About

Mackems in Sweden

We're a group of Sunderland supporters based across Sweden — expats, half-Swedes, and anyone who found their way to the lads. Whether you've been going to the Stadium of Light since Roker Park days or caught the bug from Sunderland 'Til I Die, you're welcome here.

We're registering as an official SAFC supporters branch through the Branch Liaison Council, which means we'll have a direct line to the club and a voice in supporter matters.

📺
